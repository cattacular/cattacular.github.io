A click-through gardening guide that connects people to the resources that fit their space and goals.
<!--more-->

Seed Path is an interactive flowchart for gardeners who know they want to start—but not which guides, tools, or local resources to open first. Visitors answer one question at a time (containers vs. yard, food vs. pollinators, beginner vs. leveling up) and land on curated links.

<a href="/seed-path/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open Seed Path</a>

The flow lives as a static app at `/seed-path/` on this site, so it works on GitHub Pages with no backend.
